Our Legacy

Crafted With Love in Mansa Since 1988

For nearly four decades, Sham's Sweets & Namkeen has been a household name in Mansa for authentic taste, hygienic preparation, and warm hospitality.

From wedding bhaji boxes and Diwali hampers to daily morning puri-chole and evening golgappe, our recipes preserve the timeless flavors passed down since day one.

Call for Bulk & Festive Orders
🎉 Festive & Wedding Boxes Custom assorted packs of Kaju Katli, Burfi, Milk Cake, and Namkeen.

📍 Two Convenient Locations Serving both Guru Gobind Singh Nagar (Old Court Road) and Chakerian Phatak.
